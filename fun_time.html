Proverbs on “Loaf”

“You never miss a slice from a cut loaf”: When something is plentiful, the loss of a small part of it is hardly noticed.
Example: The company donated several computers to the local school without hesitation, reasoning that you never miss a slice from a cut loaf.

“No loaf is so hard, but it may be softened in water”: No problem, person, or situation is so difficult that it cannot be improved with patience, kindness, or the right approach.
Example: Although the negotiations seemed impossible at first, the mediator persisted, believing that no loaf is so hard that it may be softened in water.
