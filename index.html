Key Takeaways


    What is a shot?
    A shot is one continuous piece of film, from one cut to the next. Every time the picture suddenly changes to a new view, a new shot begins.

    What is the difference between a long shot and a medium shot?
    A long shot shows a person’s whole body with some surroundings, while a medium shot shows a person from about the waist up, usually for conversation and gestures.

    What is a camera angle?
    We know that a camera angle is the position from which the camera looks at its subject: from above, from below, or level with it.

    Do angles always have the same effect?
    We know that the effect of an angle depends on its context. We must always explain what an angle does at that particular moment in the film, not apply a fixed rule.

    What is camera movement?
    We know that camera movement is the way the camera itself moves during a shot.

    What does a moving camera usually do to an audience?
    A moving camera can create energy and speed, and can place the audience inside the action, as if they are moving with it.

    What are the four steps for analysing a camera technique?
    We must identify the technique, locate it with a timestamp, explain its effect on the audience, and explain why the director chose it at that moment.


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Perspicuously
Meaning: In a clear, easily understood, and lucid manner.
Example Sentence: The lecturer explained the complex theory so perspicuously that even the youngest students grasped its significance.

Supervenes
Meaning: Occurs or arises unexpectedly after something else; follows as a consequence or subsequent event.
Example Sentence: A constitutional crisis often supervenes when political leaders refuse to respect established conventions.

Concatenated
Meaning: Linked together in a connected series or chain.
Example Sentence: The historian demonstrated how a series of seemingly minor events had concatenated into a major revolution.
